Superspecial isogeny graphs

Principally polarised superspecial abelian varieties over Fp² and their (ℓ,…,ℓ)-isogenies, searched from E0g with Hermitian O-lattices. Colour is the decomposition into indecomposable factors; a ring marks a Weil structure; a dot in the middle means defined over Fp.
